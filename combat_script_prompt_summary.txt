Core combat flow

- Assets/Game/UI/AbilityCaster.cs
  Central player ability script. Handles ability input, spellbook/default abilities, equipped slots, cooldowns, charge logic, aim indicators, circle/cone/rectangle telegraphs, cast routing through Mirror, generic shape damage, and ability-specific dispatch.
  This is usually the first file to inspect for player spell behavior, targeting, telegraphs, cooldown bugs, ability damage, class-specific casts, or special cases like Arcane Step.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/Health.cs
  Shared health and damage layer for players, enemies, and bosses. Owns current/max health, shields, invulnerability, damage reduction, absorption, damage redirect, healing, revive/downed state, death events, floating damage text, and enemy healthbar attachment.
  Most actual damage ends up going through Health.TakeDamage(...). If damage numbers, shields, death, healthbars, or mitigation feel wrong, inspect this file.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/StatusEffect.cs
  Defines status effect data and types: Slow, Stagger, Silenced, Cursed, Weakened, and Bound.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/StatusEffectManager.cs
  Stores active statuses, refreshes/clears them, exposes helper flags like IsSilenced/IsBound/IsWeakened, handles Cursed damage-over-time ticks, and supports debuff counting/consuming for detonation-style abilities.

- Assets/Game/Items/Scripts/CharacterStats.cs
  Aggregates gear/stat bonuses such as damage multiplier, damage reduction, cooldown reduction, movement speed, healing multiplier, and max health. AbilityCaster and Health both read from this.

Player ability helper scripts

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/DashHandler.cs
  Handles movement abilities like Arcane Step and Ironclad charge/slam behavior. Arcane Step currently moves the caster to the chosen point, while AbilityCaster handles its pulsing damage logic.

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/KineticReversalHandler.cs
  Handles Counter Blow/Kinetic Reversal style absorption and release. Uses Health absorption hooks, then releases stored damage as an area hit.

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/SiegeModeHandler.cs
  Handles Ironclad Stalwart Stance/Siege Mode behavior such as movement lock, defensive bonuses, and threat interactions.

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/IronTetherHandler.cs
  Handles Rune Chain/Iron Tether style binding and positional control.

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/StealthHandler.cs
  Handles stealth, stealth damage bonus, and enemy aggro clearing for Shadowblade style abilities.

- Assets/Game/Player/Scripts/TransferProtocolHandler.cs
  Handles damage redirect/soul bond style behavior through Health.SetDamageRedirect(...).

Ability object scripts

Enemy and boss combat

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/EnemyAI.cs
  Basic melee enemy brain. Finds players, moves toward them, attacks with Health.TakeDamage, and respects status effects like Stagger, Bound, Slow, and Silenced.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/EnemyController.cs
  Server-authoritative enemy state machine for idle/chase/attack/dead behavior. Supports melee and ranged enemies, aggro/leash ranges, ranged projectile firing, drops, and status-aware movement/attacks.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/EnemyProjectile.cs
  Ranged enemy projectile. Moves forward, damages first Player hit, then despawns.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/PlayerProjectile.cs
  Player skill-shot projectile. Moves forward, damages first Enemy hit, then despawns.

- Assets/Game/Combat/Scripts/WorldBossController.cs

Recent combat/spell indicator context from tonight

- The "rectangle not hugging ground" issue turned out to be a sanity-check mismatch: Arcanist slot 4, Collapsing Void, is a Circle ability, not a Rectangle ability. The visible square was the old circle indicator quad, not the rectangle decal path.
- Circle indicators in AbilityCaster.cs were changed from a square textured quad to a procedural circular mesh projected onto sampled/raycast ground points.
- Circle indicators now have a brighter outline LineRenderer child for readability.
- The aim placement ray now ignores actor layers/tags like Player, Enemy, and Health so AoE circles can be placed under enemies instead of stopping on their colliders.
- Arcane Step indicator size was increased from 0.5 to 3.5.
- Arcane Step now deals pulsing area damage once per second for 4 seconds, and AbilityCaster skips its old one-shot circle damage to avoid double-dipping.

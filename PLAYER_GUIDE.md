## First Five Minutes

1. **Log in**, pick a class at Character Select. Not sure? **Cleric** is the most
   forgiving, **Arcanist** the most mobile, **Ironclad** the hardest to kill.
2. You spawn in the **Hub** (Darkwood). Chat, visit the **Forge**, work a gathering
   node with **F**.
3. Press **Tab** to open your **Spellbook**. Click an ability card, then press
   **1**, **2**, **3**, or **4** to slot it. Fill all four before you fight.
4. Read each card: **delivery** (CONE / LINE / AoE / DEPLOY / SELF), **damage**,
   **range**, and the **cooldown pill** (top-right). A green **INSTANT** pill means
   no cooldown.
5. Talk to **The Hangman** (**E**) to launch an arena run. Now hold the line.

> **New-player trap:** don't slot four damage abilities. A survivable kit is roughly
> *two damage · one control/heal · one defensive or utility.* You can't DPS from the
> respawn screen.

## Controls at a Glance

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** | Move (camera-relative) |
| **Left Shift** (moving) | Sprint |
| **Space** | Jump |
| **Left Alt** / **V** | Dodge roll — 2 charges, ~0.35 s of **full invulnerability**, 5 s recharge |
| **1 – 4** | Select an equipped ability |
| **Left Mouse** | Aim & cast. **Hold to charge** — damage and area grow up to ~3× |
| **Right Mouse** / **Esc** | Cancel the aimed/held ability |
| **Right Mouse — hold & drag** | Rotate the camera (when you're *not* aiming) |
| **Mouse wheel** | Zoom in / out |
| **Tab** | Open the Spellbook (equip 1–4) |
| **F** | Gather from a node |
| **E** | Talk to an NPC (Forge, Hangman) |
| **Enter** | Chat |

**The golden rule of aiming:** your character faces where it *moves*, but abilities
fire where you *point*. Lead moving targets with skill shots — the projectile travels.

## Reading a Fight

- **Enemy telegraphs.** ~0.45 s before any enemy hits you, a **red shape** paints the
  ground. That's your dodge cue — roll *through* it, not away from it (i-frames beat
  distance).
- **Charge vs. tap.** Holding LMB charges an ability for more damage/size, but it
  roots your aim. Tap for speed when the pack is moving; charge when they're bunched
  or rooted.
- **Positioning beats DPS.** Most deaths are standing in a telegraph while greedy for
  one more cast. Cast, then move.
- **Cooldown discipline.** Watch the countdown number on your 1–4 bar. Don't open a
  fight with your 50 s ultimate — save it for the boss or the big bunch.

## The Shared Pool — 8 Abilities Everyone Can Slot

Any class can equip these (indices 0–7). They're the backbone of most kits.

| # | Ability | Type | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Runic Sentinel** *(6s)* | Deploy | Plant it **before** the pull, slightly behind your line. It auto-fires at the nearest enemy until destroyed. Free sustained DPS — never fight without one down. |
| 1 | **Void Bolt** *(3s, chargeable 15→45)* | Skill Shot | Your bread-and-butter poke. **Lead** moving targets. Tap to harass, charge when they're rooted or clustered. |
| 2 | **Mending Circle** *(5s)* | AoE Heal | Drop it on the spot the team will stand — chokes, boss melee range. Route *through* its edge; the afterglow follows you out. |
| 3 | **Storm Lash** *(4s, 15→50)* | Line | Sweeps everything in a straight path. Aim it down the lane a pack is funneling into. Great charged into a bunched line. |
| 4 | **Ember Surge** *(4s, 20→45)* | AoE | Placed burst. Tap it to apply Burning for setup; charge it for the big detonation. |
| 5 | **Mind Spike** *(5s, 35)* | AoE | Single-target nuke. Save it for elites/priority targets, not trash. |
| 6 | **Binding Wave** *(6s, 15)* | AoE | Damage **+ root**. This is control, not damage — use it to lock a pack for the team's AoE, or to peel a chaser off the healer. |
| 7 | **Arcane Ward** *(8s, 50 absorb)* | Self | Pop it **before** you take a hit you can see coming (a telegraph, a boss cast). Reactive shields are wasted shields. |

## Hero Playbooks

Each hero has a **passive**, a signature **opener**, a core **rotation**, and a short
list of **don'ts**. Slot the shared pool around these.

### Warden — the Construct Tactician

*"Hold the line. The constructs will do the rest."* · **Class 0** · Battlemage / Utility

**Passive — Runic Mastery:** every construct you control raises your ability damage.
More turrets = more everything.

**Signature abilities**
| Ability | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Runic Snare** *(5s, 40)* | Place it on a chokepoint or under the boss. It's a mark *and* a mine — enemies that cross it eat a burst and take more construct damage after. |
| **Battle Hymn** *(12s)* | Team cooldown reduction. Sing it when the party is *burning* cooldowns, not before — you want people mid-rotation to benefit. |
| **Spirit Redirect** *(8s)* | Command your Sentinel to focus-fire your target. Use it to dump construct damage onto a pre-stacked priority target. |
| **Mend** *(6s)* | Single-target heal + full debuff purge. It can cleanse the Scorched root — a niche the Cleric's Dispel doesn't cover. |
| **Conjurer's Surge** *(45s)* | Ultimate: fires **every** active construct at once. Plant everything first, *then* surge. |

**Opener:** Sentinel + Snare down before the pull → poke with Void Bolt → Spirit
Redirect onto the elite → Battle Hymn as the team commits → Conjurer's Surge on the
big bunch.

**Don't:** surge with only one turret out. Don't stand on your own Snare.

### Ironclad — the Iron Vanguard

*"Let them hit me. I'm building a debt they can't pay."* · **Class 1** · Tank / Disruptor

**Passive — Iron Will:** each hit taken in Stalwart Stance builds Fortify, cutting
Shieldwall Charge's cooldown. Tanking *is* your resource generation.

**Signature abilities**
| Ability | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Shieldwall Charge** *(6s, 25)* | Your engage. Charge *through* the pack to generate Threat on everyone — and drive enemies into an Iron Rampart for the Exposed debuff. |
| **Gravity Slam** *(7s)* | Pulls a pack into one point. This is the team's "bunch them up" button — call it so the AoE follows. |
| **Counter Blow** *(10s, up to 60)* | Absorb stance for 3 s, release as a cone of everything you soaked. Hold it while the boss is swinging, release into their face. |
| **Stalwart Stance** *(14s)* | 40% damage reduction + tripled Threat, but you can't move. Plant it when you *want* to be hit — during a boss's targeted phase. |
| **Rune Chain** *(9s)* | Leash an enemy: 15% of their damage to allies is absorbed, and Tension builds for a free stagger on expiry. Chain the scariest hitter. |
| **Iron Rampart** *(50s)* | A wall that blocks **all** projectiles for 10 s. Split a ranged encounter, or wall off a lane during a burn. |

**Opener:** Shieldwall Charge in → Gravity Slam to bunch → Stalwart Stance and hold →
Counter Blow into the boss → Rampart when the ranged wave arrives.

**Don't:** Stance up while mobile mechanics are chasing you — you can't dodge rooted.

### Shadowblade (Bo-gar) — the Void Infiltrator

*"They can't hit what they can't see. Or silence."* · **Class 2** · Assassin / Disruptor

**Passive — Phantom Discipline:** landing a hit *from stealth* resets Shadow Veil once
per veil. Your burst loops if you open correctly.

**Signature abilities**
| Ability | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Shadow Veil** *(10s)* | Vanish 4 s. Breaking stealth immediately with **Mind Spike** deals +50%. That combo is your signature opener. |
| **Silence Ward** *(12s)* | A fog that **silences** enemy abilities and applies Cursed. Drop it on caster packs or on the boss during a dangerous cast window. |
| **Dark Harvest** *(40s)* | Consumes all debuff stacks on nearby enemies for **20 damage per stack**. This is a finisher — it's only huge if the target is *pre-stacked* (Cursed, Void Rot). |

**Opener:** Shadow Veil → Mind Spike burst (+50%) → Silence Ward on the pack →
stack Cursed → **Dark Harvest** when stacks are high. At 4+ Cursed, Harvest also
applies the Withered slow — worth more than raw damage at boss phase transitions.

**Don't:** Harvest early. Empty stacks = wasted 40 s ultimate. Let the team seed decay
first (see [Party Combos](#party-combos-that-win)).

### Cleric (Brandolf) — the Soul Warden

*"The fight ends when I say it ends."* · **Class 3** · Support / Healer · *beginner-friendly*

**Passive — Grace Under Fire:** below 30% HP, all your heals tick at **double** rate
for 8 s. You are strongest when it looks worst — don't panic-disengage.

**Signature abilities**
| Ability | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Spirit Wisps** *(7s)* | Drifting seekers that heal allies (and chip enemies they pass). Keep them on cooldown — it's your steady background healing. |
| **Sacred Aegis** *(10s)* | A shield that **grows** as the target takes hits (up to 80). Put it on whoever's about to eat damage — the tank in Stance, the carry diving in. |
| **Soul Bond** *(9s)* | Reroute an ally's incoming damage onto **you** for 5 s. Sacrifice play — bond the squishy carry through a burst window (you have the passive to survive it). |
| **Divine Spark** *(14s)* | **Revives** a downed ally at 30% — or nukes undead for 60. Your most important button; never waste it on damage if someone might go down. |
| **Dispel** *(7s)* | Purges the single most dangerous debuff on an ally. Surgical — use it on transition states (Scorched, Void Silence). |
| **Temporal Grace** *(60s)* | Ultimate: rewinds the **whole team** 5 s — HP, positions, debuffs. The strongest ability in the game. Save it for a wipe-in-progress. |

**Rotation:** keep Wisps rolling → Aegis the incoming-damage target → Soul Bond the
carry on big hits → hold Divine Spark for revives → **never** blow Temporal Grace early.

**Don't:** over-heal healthy people. Watch positions and pre-shield instead of
reacting.

### Arcanist — the Void Mage

*"Distance is an illusion. So is the concept of 'safe.'"* · **Class 4** · Mage / Control

**Passive — Phase Resonance:** each **Arcane Step** within 4 s of the last cuts Void
Maw's cooldown (stacks ×3). Blink often to keep your control online.

**Signature abilities**
| Ability | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Arcane Step** *(4s)* | A **true blink** — through terrain, colliders, hitboxes. Reposition, dodge a telegraph, or flank. Its echo also seeds decay on a group you blink through. |
| **Void Maw** *(9s, 20)* | Opens a singularity that **pulls** for 3 s then detonates. Your engage — drop it on a pack, then chain lightning the clump. |
| **Forked Lightning** *(7s, 30 chain)* | Arcs between up to 4 enemies. Best value into a bunched group (thanks, Void Maw). |
| **Collapsing Void** *(50s, 60)* | Ultimate event horizon: pulls 3 s, collapses for 60 AoE + Weakened. Land it when the **whole** pack is grouped. |

**Opener:** Arcane Step to flank → Void Maw to pull → Forked Lightning the clump →
blink out when they turn → Collapsing Void when the team has them stacked.

**Don't:** blink *into* the middle of a pack with no exit. Distance is your defense.

## Party Combos That Win

The best plays live in the seams between kits. A few that matter:

| Combo | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Arcanist → Shadowblade** | Void Bolts and Arcane Step seed **Void Rot / Void Leak**; **Dark Harvest** now eats those stacks. Pre-seed a target, then Harvest for a huge finisher. |
| **Ironclad → everyone** | **Gravity Slam** bunches the pack; Arcanist's Forked Lightning, Warden's turrets, and Ember Surge all hit the clump at full value. |
| **Ironclad → Arcanist** | **Rune Chain** holds a target 5 s — a clean window for 3 Void Bolts → Void Rot ×3 → Binding Wave → **Void Collapse**. |
| **Warden + Arcanist** | Arcanist pre-stacks Void Rot; Warden **Spirit Redirects** all Sentinels onto that target (+bonus per stack). Add **Conjurer's Surge** = highest single-target burst in the game. |
| **Cleric + Ironclad** | **Sacred Aegis** on the Ironclad while he **Counter Blow** absorbs; Aegis breaks into Sacred Regeneration as he releases. Layered survivability through the burst. |
| **Arcanist + Shadowblade** | Arcane Step echo seeds **Void Leak** on a group; Shadowblade's **Silence Ward** on the same spot doubles the Cursed ticks (**Void Silence**). Highest sustained AoE. |

> **One rule for the whole party:** don't let **Mending Circle** cleanse enemy debuffs.
> A badly-placed heal zone can wipe the Shadowblade's carefully-built stacks. Zone
> cleanses allies only — place it on your team, not on the enemy pile.

## Boss Survival Notes

- **The Null Architect** — Phase 1 has a **Reflect** window every 18 s: *stop attacking*
  when the warning fires, or your own damage bounces back at 75%. In Phase 2, burst the
  three shards **together** (they cross-heal). Phase 3, stack for **Void Drain**, spread
  for **Tether**. Save cooldowns for Final Surge at 10%.
- **The Iron Warden** — punishes passivity. Clear its **Magnetized** DoT by standing in
  a Mending Circle for 1.5 s. Void Rot pre-stacked on an arm carries through its respawn
  immunity — the intended Spirit Redirect window.
- **General:** every boss reads a state off your party and reacts. Cleanse *before* the
  enrage/surge, not during. When in doubt, dodge first and DPS second.

## Getting Stronger — Between Runs

Three parallel tracks — push all three:

- **Hero Mastery** — earned clearing waves and bosses. Unlocks per-hero passive bonuses
  (damage, healing, cooldown, HP) through your stats. Play the hero to power the hero.
- **Professions** — **Mining / Woodcutting / Fishing**. Press **F** at a node and go
  idle; the game rewards you on a tick. Feeds the Forge.
- **Gear** — drops from combat + **Forge** crafting. Some of the strongest tools (boss
  resist flasks) are **craft-only** and never drop. Craft, don't just farm.

Power in Crossworlds comes from **gear + mastery + skill** — not from a level number.
The best upgrade is usually you reading telegraphs faster.

## Ten Habits of a Good Crossworlds Player

1. **Always have a Sentinel down** before the pull.
2. **Dodge through telegraphs**, not away — i-frames beat footspeed.
3. **Pre-shield and pre-cleanse**; reacting is a half-second too late.
4. **Bunch, then blast** — control (Gravity Slam, Void Maw, Binding Wave) before AoE.
5. **Save ultimates** for the boss or the big bunch, not the first trash wave.
6. **Lead your skill shots.** The projectile travels; the enemy moves.
7. **Cast, then move.** Standing still to squeeze one more shot is how you die.
8. **Watch the cooldown numbers** on your 1–4 bar and plan the next three casts.
9. **Talk to your team** — call Gravity Slams, Harvest windows, and Temporal Grace.
10. **Don't greed.** A live hero out of position beats a dead hero mid-combo.
